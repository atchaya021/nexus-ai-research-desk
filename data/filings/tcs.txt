TATA CONSULTANCY SERVICES — Q1 FY27 EARNINGS SUMMARY (SIMULATED FILING)

Revenue and Growth
Constant-currency revenue growth was 4.2% year-on-year, at the lower end of management's historical
guidance range. Growth was led by BFSI and Manufacturing verticals, while Retail and North America
Communications remained soft due to continued discretionary spend caution among clients.

Deal Pipeline
Total contract value (TCV) bookings softened sequentially, with management attributing this to elongated
decision cycles among large US enterprise clients amid macro uncertainty. TCS did win a large multi-year
BFSI transformation deal in Europe during the quarter, partially offsetting the softer US pipeline.

Profitability and Margins
Operating margin remained industry-leading at 19.6%, supported by continued utilization discipline and
pyramid optimization, though management noted incremental wage-hike costs will pressure margin in the
subsequent quarter.

People Metrics
Voluntary attrition ticked up modestly quarter-on-quarter to 13.2% on a trailing-twelve-month basis,
still well below pandemic-era peaks but a data point worth monitoring for wage cost trajectory.

Balance Sheet
The company remains effectively debt-free with a debt-to-equity ratio of 0.08x, and continues its
practice of returning a high proportion of free cash flow to shareholders via dividends and buybacks.

Valuation Context
Trailing P/E of approximately 27.9x sits modestly above the IT services sector average of 26.0x,
reflecting TCS's premium for scale, margin stability, and diversified client base.

Risk Factors Disclosed
- Client budget caution in North America and discretionary spend deferrals
- Currency (USD/INR) volatility affecting reported revenue growth
- Wage inflation and attrition-driven cost pressure
- Increasing competitive intensity in AI-led service delivery models
