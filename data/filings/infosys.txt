INFOSYS LIMITED — Q1 FY27 EARNINGS SUMMARY (SIMULATED FILING)

Revenue and Growth
Revenue grew 6.1% year-on-year in constant currency terms, exceeding the upper end of prior guidance.
Management raised full-year revenue growth guidance, citing strong large-deal signings across Financial
Services and Manufacturing, and continued momentum in generative AI-linked engagements.

Deal Wins
Infosys expanded its generative AI partnership with a global banking client during the quarter, and
reported total large-deal TCV ahead of the year-ago comparable period, indicating a healthier near-term
revenue visibility than some peers.

Profitability and Margins
Operating margin came in at 17.2%, with reported margin slightly impacted by adverse cross-currency
movements. Management reiterated its stated operating margin band and indicated confidence in expanding
margin through the remainder of the fiscal year via operating leverage.

Balance Sheet
Debt-to-equity remains low at 0.10x. The company maintains a strong net cash position and continues a
consistent capital return program via dividends and buybacks.

Valuation Context
Trailing P/E of approximately 25.3x is slightly below the IT services sector average of 26.0x, which some
analysts view as attractive given the upgraded growth guidance relative to large-cap IT peers.

Risk Factors Disclosed
- Currency headwinds affecting reported (though not constant-currency) revenue and margin
- Client concentration risk in a small number of top accounts
- Pricing pressure in commoditized application-maintenance services
- Talent and wage-cost inflation, particularly in AI/data specialist roles
