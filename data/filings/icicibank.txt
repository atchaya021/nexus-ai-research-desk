ICICI BANK LIMITED — Q1 FY27 EARNINGS SUMMARY (SIMULATED FILING)

Growth and Loan Book
Retail loan book growth remained strong, with management citing broad-based momentum across mortgages,
vehicle loans, and unsecured retail credit. Overall advances growth outpaced the banking sector average
for the quarter.

Earnings Performance
The bank reported an earnings beat relative to street estimates, driven by strong net interest income
growth and healthy fee income. Several brokerages raised target prices following the results.

Asset Quality
Gross and net non-performing asset ratios improved further, continuing a multi-quarter trend of asset
quality strengthening. Provisioning remains conservative relative to reported stress levels.

Profitability and Margins
Net interest margin held up better than peers in the current rate cycle, supported by a favorable
funding mix and continued growth in the low-cost current and savings account (CASA) deposit base.

Balance Sheet
Debt-to-equity (funding structure typical for a bank) stands at 0.79x. Capital ratios remain well above
regulatory minimums, providing headroom for continued loan growth.

Valuation Context
Trailing P/E of approximately 21.2x is at a premium to the banking sector average of 18.4x. Some analysts
have flagged that valuation now appears stretched following the recent share price rally, even as
fundamentals remain strong.

Risk Factors Disclosed
- Valuation risk following a sustained rally relative to sector peers
- Unsecured retail credit growth warrants continued underwriting discipline
- Macro sensitivity of retail asset quality to a slowdown in consumption
- Competitive intensity in deposit mobilization affecting funding costs
