STATE BANK OF INDIA — Q1 FY27 EARNINGS SUMMARY (SIMULATED FILING)

Growth and Loan Book
Advances grew across corporate, retail, and SME segments, with management citing
continued momentum in home loans and SME lending as key drivers of loan book expansion.

Profitability
Net profit grew year-on-year on the back of strong net interest income and controlled
credit costs. Return on assets continued its gradual improvement trend.

Asset Quality
Gross non-performing asset ratio improved further. Management flagged select
agricultural loan segments as warranting continued monitoring given regional
weather-related repayment risk.

Digital Initiatives
The bank highlighted continued growth in its digital lending platform and YONO app
user base as a driver of lower-cost customer acquisition.

Balance Sheet
Debt-to-equity (typical bank funding structure) stands at 0.91x. As India's largest
public sector bank, capital adequacy remains comfortably above regulatory minimums,
supported by periodic capital raises.

Valuation Context
Trailing P/E of approximately 11.4x is well below the banking sector average of
18.4x, consistent with the historical PSU-bank valuation discount versus private
sector peers, even as fundamentals have strengthened in recent years.

Risk Factors Disclosed
- Asset quality sensitivity in agricultural and select MSME loan segments
- Execution risk on digital transformation initiatives
- Government ownership and policy-linked lending considerations
- Competitive intensity from private sector banks in retail and digital lending
