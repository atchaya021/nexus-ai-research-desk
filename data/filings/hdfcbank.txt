HDFC BANK LIMITED — Q1 FY27 EARNINGS SUMMARY (SIMULATED FILING)

Growth and Loan Book
Deposit growth of 10.4% year-on-year outpaced credit growth for the third consecutive quarter, a trend
management describes as a deliberate strategy to improve the loan-to-deposit ratio following the HDFC
merger integration.

Asset Quality
Gross non-performing assets remained stable quarter-on-quarter, and provisioning coverage ratio improved
modestly, reflecting continued conservative underwriting standards across retail and commercial segments.

Profitability and Margins
Net interest margin faced continued compression pressure amid the prevailing rate cycle, though management
indicated NIM should stabilize as the deposit repricing cycle catches up with the asset side over the next
two to three quarters.

Balance Sheet
Debt-to-equity (including borrowings typical for a bank's funding structure) stands at 0.85x. Capital
adequacy ratio remains comfortably above regulatory requirements.

Valuation Context
Trailing P/E of approximately 19.6x compares to a banking sector average of 18.4x, a modest premium
attributed to HDFC Bank's scale, deposit franchise, and post-merger integration progress.

Risk Factors Disclosed
- Net interest margin compression risk during the ongoing rate cycle
- Integration execution risk from the HDFC Ltd merger
- Asset quality sensitivity to macroeconomic slowdown in unsecured retail lending
- Regulatory changes affecting priority-sector lending and liquidity coverage requirements
