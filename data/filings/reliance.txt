RELIANCE INDUSTRIES LIMITED — Q1 FY27 EARNINGS SUMMARY (SIMULATED FILING)

Revenue and Growth
Consolidated revenue grew 8.9% year-on-year, driven primarily by the Retail and Digital Services segments.
Oil-to-Chemicals (O2C) revenue was largely flat as refining margins normalized from prior-year highs.
Jio Platforms added 4.2 million net subscribers during the quarter, with ARPU improving to Rs 203.

Profitability and Margins
Consolidated EBITDA margin was 8.4%, broadly stable quarter-on-quarter.
Management flagged near-term margin pressure in the O2C segment due to elevated crude input costs
and softer polymer spreads in export markets. This pressure is expected to persist for one to two
additional quarters before easing as new capacity ramps.

Balance Sheet
Net debt-to-equity stood at 0.41x, within management's stated comfort range of below 0.5x.
The company continues to fund capital expenditure for its green hydrogen and new energy business
primarily through internal accruals, supplemented by selective strategic stake sales.

Growth Initiatives
Reliance Retail expanded its quick-commerce footprint to 40 additional cities during the quarter.
The company commissioned a pilot green hydrogen production facility at its Jamnagar complex, positioning
the New Energy vertical as a multi-year growth driver, though near-term revenue contribution remains small.

Valuation Context
Trailing P/E of approximately 24.8x compares to a diversified conglomerate sector average of 22.1x,
reflecting a premium attributed to growth optionality in Retail, Digital Services, and New Energy.

Risk Factors Disclosed
- Crude oil price volatility affecting O2C segment profitability
- Regulatory and spectrum-related costs in telecom operations
- Execution risk on large capital expenditure programs in New Energy
- Currency fluctuation exposure given significant import content in refining feedstock
